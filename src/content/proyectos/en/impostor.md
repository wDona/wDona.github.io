---
title: "The Impostor (word game)"
summary: "The viral word game known as the impostor."
---

It's a game where there is a word everyone knows, except for one person (the impostor).

Each player must say a related word so the others know they aren't the impostor, but without making it easy for the impostor to guess the word.
The impostor has to blend in as if they knew the word, so on their turn they also have to say a word they think is related (without knowing what it is).

Once everyone has said a related word, those who know the word have to deduce who the impostor is from the words that were said. If nobody is suspicious, the game continues with another round of words. If many are suspicious, there is a voting phase to pick who they think the impostor is.

The most voted player reveals their role (impostor or innocent), and if they are the impostor, they get a chance to guess the word. If they get it right, the impostor wins; if not, the innocents win.

Over time, variants have appeared on social media that give it a fresh twist.

I made this app because I wanted to play the impostor with friends online and there were no apps on the Play Store or websites to play it online, so I built it myself.
