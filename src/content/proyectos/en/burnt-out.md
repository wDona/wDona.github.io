---
title: "Burn't out"
summary: "Gamified Trello that encourages teamwork and prevents Burnout Syndrome."
---

Mobile and desktop app for assigning and tracking tasks in work teams while promoting well-being, aimed at preventing Burnout Syndrome.

It's my cross-platform app development capstone project, built in Kotlin with Jetpack Compose for the UI and Ktor for the API.
